# Surrounding-town content research — 23 September 2026

## Scope and evidence limits

The owner confirmed that the business serves the towns and villages around Inverness and Aberdeen. This is operational coverage confirmation, not proof of a driver at every location at every time. The owner's fleet-size and proximity statements have not been independently verified and are not published in the new copy. There is no seven-minute arrival promise, local-office claim, guaranteed availability, invented price or ranking guarantee.

This increment adds five canonical town records in the existing `AREAS` data: Nairn, Dingwall, Westhill in Aberdeenshire, Stonehaven and Ellon. It expands the existing Inverurie record with practical guidance and FAQs. It does not create a service-by-town page matrix or a separate page for every village. More surrounding settlements belong in useful coverage sections on the established city hubs until a separate page has sufficient distinct purpose and evidence.

Research used public web-search results and directly opened pages on 23 September 2026. Observed results below mean that a search query returned the named page. They are not a controlled Google ranking measurement, a Search Console query export, evidence of query volume or proof of a competitor's operational claims. No search volumes were inferred. Search-engine crawl timestamps and content can differ from the page opened directly.

## Direct competitor-page observations

- [Man with Van Network removals](https://www.manwithvannetwork.co.uk/removals) visibly groups Nairn, Dingwall and other Highland communities and discusses rural approaches and load types. That supports describing the actual surrounding service patch. It does not establish the owner's fleet, work history or ability to serve any given date.
- [Hello Vans Westhill](https://hellovans.com/man-with-a-van/westhill/) has service categories, FAQs, price tiers and neighbouring-town links. Its local data section also showed unresolved placeholders such as “See area postcodes” when inspected. The new page uses a verified broad postcode example and useful local distinctions. No competitor prices, ratings, review text or time promises were copied.
- [The Ellon man-with-a-van page](https://man-and-van-hire.com/man-with-a-van-ellon) discusses load, access and crew, then links to many service-specific pages. The new Ellon record addresses those decisions on one page. Its cancellation, reply-time and same-day claims have not been adopted.
- [URemovals Stonehaven](https://uremovals.com/man-and-van/stonehaven) displays individual provider entries, distances and reviews. Those are published competitor statements, not independently audited local completed jobs. Equivalent trust evidence would require genuine permissioned business records; this increment does not invent testimonials or completed moves.
