SpeedyVan Admin is an internal operations app for SpeedyVan administrators.

Demo credentials:
Email: admin@speedyvan.com
Password: Admin123!

The app requires sign-in. After login:
- Dashboard shows operational KPIs.
- Bookings lets admins search, filter, open booking details, change status, assign drivers, add tracking notes, and cancel bookings.
- Drivers lets admins review driver profiles, active status, earnings, mark unpaid earnings as paid, and reset passwords.
- Jobs lets admins control available job visibility, pause/publish jobs, and set driver pay.
- More links to Analytics, Enquiries, and Notifications.

The app uses standard HTTPS communication with the SpeedyVan API at:
https://www.speedyvan.uk/api

No non-exempt encryption is used beyond standard HTTPS.
